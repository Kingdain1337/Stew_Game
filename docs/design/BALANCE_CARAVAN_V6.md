# Caravan balance diagnostics — rules v6

Provisional targets: **550 / 900 / 1500 / 1300 / 2200 / 2100 / 3500** over seven five-bowl encounters. Boss targets spike at chapters 3, 5 and 7; the following ordinary chapter gives room to rebuild. These targets are story-owned, independent of pot choice, and do not change the ten-chapter royal curve.

Five seeds (1–5) per configuration, with Pantry plus one themed deck. Two bounded bot policies compare immediate bowl maximization with an engine-aware policy that can save ingredients and bank resources. This is not a player win rate or evidence that the story is fun.

The alternatives can win but ask for more long-term planning; Hearth is easier, especially in the Royal pot. The final dragon is the main differentiator. Bandit variety is tracked from non-system ingredient types that actually score during the encounter. The first chapter draws only forage-friendly stocks and offer types; later chapters use the selected deck's full pool.

Next: human playtests should check whether the first two chapters are engaging despite low pressure, whether three-type bandit variety is readable, whether the troll's large-bowl restriction feels fair, and whether the dragon trial rewards earlier engine-building without requiring a narrow strategy. The diagnostic bot is not an optimal or representative player.
